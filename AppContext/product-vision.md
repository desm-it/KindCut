# Product Vision

Build a grandma-easy local-first Cricut companion app for Windows/macOS.

The app should let a user describe a craft idea, generate a Cricut-ready SVG, preview cut/draw layers, choose machine/material/mat/tool settings, save the design locally, and eventually send it to Cricut via SliceBug.

Primary wedge: Cricut Joy cards and simple pen+cut projects.
